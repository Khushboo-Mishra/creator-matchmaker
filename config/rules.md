# Campaign rules

PLACEHOLDER. These five headings are a contract: `src/score_ai.py` reads this file
and the headings must not change. The content below is throwaway so the pipeline
runs today. Replace the content, keep the structure.

## Product

A mineral sunscreen, SPF 40, unscented, for daily use.

## Audience

25 to 40, urban, buys skincare online, reads ingredient lists.

## Message

Everyday protection that does not feel like sunscreen.

## Allowed claims

- Broad spectrum SPF 40
- Non-comedogenic
- Reef-safe formulation
- Water resistant for 40 minutes

## Banned claims

- Any claim that the product prevents or treats skin cancer
- Any claim about reversing signs of ageing
- Any comparison naming a competitor product
- Any implication that reapplication is unnecessary

## Disclosure format

Spoken disclosure in the first 30 seconds plus "#ad" as the first characters
of the video description.
